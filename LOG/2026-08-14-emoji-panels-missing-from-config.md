# 67a7dd0 — The key config and its generated manifest now say the emoji panels are deliberately outside their scope

The key config described itself as the single source of truth for which characters exist and where they sit, with no carve-out. SPEC's manifest principle said the same. Emoji are characters on panels, and five emoji panels exist in the prototype and in SPEC — so on a plain reading of both documents they belonged in the config and were missing from it. That tension was noticed on 2026-08-07 while checking the emoji panel count.

Two readings were available and the fork was real rather than an oversight. Either the emoji were a gap in a manifest that should cover them, or the manifest was never meant to reach them because emoji panels are system-supplied content rather than a hand-curated inventory. The user chose the second at the /plan session of 2026-08-14, and SPEC was edited there to scope its manifest principle to the three letter and symbol panels. The first reading loses because the four manifest rules — no key lost, no unresolved duplicates, no silent changes, empty slots are opportunities — only make sense over an inventory somebody curates by hand. There is no meaningful sense in which a slot is "freed" in a system-supplied emoji panel, so applying the rules there would be ceremony over content nobody controls.

What was left after that /plan session was the same sentence in the two places SPEC does not reach, and it was kept as build work rather than done in planning because it touches a config, a script and a generated file. The config's `about` field now states the scope and the carve-out. The generator's intro prose carries it too — necessarily separately, because that prose is hard-coded in the script rather than read from `about`, so the two would otherwise drift apart. The manifest itself was regenerated by re-running the script, never hand-edited, which is the rule the file states about itself.

The result is that a reader who counts three panels in the config and five in SPEC now finds the discrepancy explained in the config, in the manifest, and in SPEC, rather than having to infer intent from an absence.

**Files touched:**
- resources/key-layout.json: the `about` field gained a sentence scoping the file to the three letter and symbol panels and naming the emoji panels as system-supplied and deliberately outside it
- scripts/generate-key-manifest.py: the same carve-out added to the hard-coded intro prose in `build()`, ahead of the geometry paragraph and the four-rules heading, stating that their absence is correct rather than a gap
- resources/key-manifest.md: regenerated from the script, picking up the two new lines
