# f83f5d9 — [soft-edge-fraction-values] the two fill fractions shipped at 0.75 and 1.045, then judged too thin on the phone the same session

This session ran across 2026-09-05 and 2026-09-09.

The item existed because the soft key edge, built on 2026-09-04, made the keys read *smaller*: the part of a key that looks definitely there had fallen from 0.86 of the touch radius — where the old hard disc effectively sat — to `SOLID_FRACTION = 0.55`, and labels are a fixed fraction of that, so the glyphs shrank by a third at the same moment and for the same reason. The user chose a picked value shipped with the install over a comparison affordance on the phone, so the build's whole job was to apply two numbers and write down where they came from.

`SOLID_FRACTION` goes to 0.75 and `FADE_FRACTION` to 1.045. The second is the one worth keeping the derivation for, and the code's own comment had it wrong: it named "about 1.18, where neighbouring fades just meet". Neighbouring key centres sit exactly one vertical step apart — `2 * radius + gap(radius)`, and the gap is `max(1.5, radius * 0.09)` — so above a solved radius of about 16.7dp the centres are 2.09 radii apart and half that is 1.045. At 1.18 the fades overlap substantially. Below 16.7dp the 1.5dp floor takes over and the centres sit proportionally further apart, so the fades fall short of meeting rather than running into each other, which makes 1.045 a ceiling at every radius the board can solve rather than a value that happens to work at one. That arithmetic is now beside the constants, and `KeyEdgeTest` asserts it across the whole solvable range at half-dp steps rather than at three sample radii — three samples could sit entirely on one side of the crossover.

**The values were then looked at, which is the part this entry exists to record.** The walkthrough of 2026-09-09 put them on the Pixel 6 and the verdict was that they are wrong: the outer size of the circles is right and the gradient is too short, so the fade should extend further inwards rather than outwards — which reads directly onto `SOLID_FRACTION` being too high — and the labels are too small. That is the item working rather than failing. Its own walkthrough step said an opinion either way was the result, and the opinion arrived. It is carried forward as [key-drawing-second-pass], along with the user's argument that a label should be sized against the key as an object rather than against a box drawn inside it — an argument that reverses [soft-key-edge]'s deliberate decision, and was put to him as such rather than quietly adopted.
