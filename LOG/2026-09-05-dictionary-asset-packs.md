# 5f9d97e — [dictionary-asset-packs] filed for the delivery half of the two-speed language plan

Filed 2026-09-05 from the user's question about download size; [language-list-choice]'s record for the same date carries the plan.

The property that made the route acceptable at all: Google Play performs the delivery and the app makes no network request of its own, so a keyboard can gain assets after install without an internet permission or a server. The price is publishing as an App Bundle through Play, which a sideloaded build cannot use.

Stated rather than glossed, and worth re-reading before it is built: Android's documentation frames asset packs as a games feature, no non-game example was read, and the size limits were noted as existing rather than read. Held behind [predictive-dictionary-bundle] because the mechanism only earns its complexity at the second dictionary, and there is no second dictionary until a language's word list has been found and its licence read.
