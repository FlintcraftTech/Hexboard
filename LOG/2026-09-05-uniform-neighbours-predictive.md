The three caveats the item said to settle first all turned out to be desk answers. A key's neighbours are the keys one step away, so an edge key simply has fewer than six — uniformity is a property interior keys have, not a requirement the distance measure imposes. The two space bars are excluded, because a tap landing on space ends the word and ending the word is the correction moment. And neighbour sets are per panel, so a mis-tap cannot cross a swipe boundary.

The frequency half was closed by reading rather than reasoning.
